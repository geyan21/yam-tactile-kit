Touch sensing that plugs into the YAM gripper

Each finger is a cartridge: a flat {{cols}} × {{rows}} taxel pad under a replaceable TPU skin, its own readout board, and five pogo pins to the robot. It docks with two thumbscrews and keeps the stock fingertip position.

A flat bay instead of a curved fingertip

The ENPIRE-style tip curves in two directions at once. Polyimide does not stretch, so no flat circuit can be folded onto it, which is why unfolding the gripper STL into a custom FPC kept failing.

This design moves the irregular shape into the printed core and the skin, and gives the sensor one flat face. The pad is a plain rectangle, and every trace leaves through the base, away from the narrow tip.

The cartridge keeps the stock tool point at z = {{tcp_z}} mm, so existing configs and IK targets still work.

Parts to show

    How it goes together

    1. The socket replaces the stock tip on the carriage, using the same heat-set inserts and screws, and carries a small pogo-pin board. It stays on the robot.
    2. The sensor pad, an FPC, Velostat and FPC laminate, sits flush in the cartridge's flat bay. Its two tails run up a groove and into the finger PCB.
    3. The finger PCB digitises all {{taxels}} taxels inside the cartridge and talks USB to the host over five pogo pins.
    4. The TPU skin presses onto the face. Ribs on its side walls snap into grooves, so payload shear can't walk it off the tip.
    5. To dock, push the cartridge straight up onto two dowels and hand-tighten two thumbscrews. Swapping a finger needs no tools.

    {{taxels}} taxels, digitised inside the finger

    Rows and columns are copper electrodes on two FPCs with Velostat between them; each crossing is a taxel. The layout follows the FlexiTac V2 process.

    Pogo pins

    The ground pins are longer, so ground connects first when you dock a cartridge with the robot powered.

    Frame format

    AA 55verflagsseqt_us{{taxels}} × u16 pressureCRC

    268 bytes per frame, little endian, row-major with row 0 at the tip. The checksum is CRC-16/CCITT-FALSE. Tail pins 9–16 carry rows 15 down to 8, and host/tactile_tools.py puts them back in order.

    Checked against the robot's own geometry

    Every number here is measured from the exported parts and i2rt's YAM meshes by cad/check_fit.py and cad/measure.py.

    • At least {{clear_opp}} mm of clearance to the opposite carriage, and {{clear_housing}} mm to the housing, across the full 0–47.5 mm stroke.
    • Walls of at least {{wall_pad}} mm behind the pad, and {{wall_min_hole}}–{{wall_max_hole}} mm around every insert and screw hole.
    • The only overlaps are intended ones: the skins squeeze {{squeeze}} mm each at full close, the pogo pins compress 0.7 mm, and the dowels press-fit.
    • In MuJoCo, a 24 mm block held at a {{sim_grip}} N grip reads {{sim_left}} N on each finger across {{sim_taxels}} taxels.
    • An independent review of v0.1 found the socket scraping the opposite carriage below about 23 mm of opening. v{{version}} drops that wing by {{relief}} mm, as the stock tip does.

    Not verified yet

    Nothing has been printed, fitted to a real gripper, or loaded. Pad sensitivity, drift, skin wear and the finger PCB firmware are still open.

    Before you print a batch

    1. Confirm that the stock tip screws go in from above, through the carriage, into the tip's heat-set inserts. The socket copies that arrangement.
    2. Measure a laminated pad. It should sit flush in the {{pad_t}} mm pocket, or at most 0.05 mm proud.
    3. Check the ZIF keep-outs and the FPC entry height against the connectors you buy.
    4. Print one socket and one cartridge. Check the dowel fit, the skin's snap, and the {{squeeze}} mm squeeze at full close.

    Printing

    Print the cartridge with the docking face on the bed and the finger pointing up, with no supports: only the PCB seat bridges. Use 0.2 mm layers, four or five walls and at least 40% infill, in PETG or PA-CF. The socket prints on its carriage face, and the skin on its contact face in TPU 85A.

    Keep the gripper force limit at about 80 N or below.

    Rebuild everything

    cd cartridge
pip install -r requirements.txt
make i2rt    # i2rt meshes at the measured commit
make all     # CAD, FPC, checks, sim, renders, this page

    The host tools in host/tactile_tools.py decode the frame stream, lift taxels to 3D points for visuo-tactile fusion, and handle bench bring-up on a FlexiTac readout board.
