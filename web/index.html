Recovered normal force—

Subtracting the moments cancels the changing distal lever arm in this model. A real design still needs matched sensing, calibration, moment rejection, and a stiffness budget.

Normal force does not detect every lateral contact. Noise, temperature, weight, and dynamic loads are not modeled here.
