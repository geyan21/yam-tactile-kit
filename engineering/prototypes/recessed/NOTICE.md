# B3.1 experimental recessed prototype

Original ENPIRE geometry: Wenli Xiao / NVIDIA ENPIRE. Source provenance and pinned meshes are documented in engineering/docs/GEOMETRY.md. This frame is a faceted derivative of the source STL, not the author's original analytic CAD.

B3 supplies a separately recessed PETG frame and tapered locating spacers. It retains the B2.1 PCB and removable backer shape, B1 TPU/magnets and R1 N0 nail cells. Its firmware uses gain 5 and protocol yam-b3/1; physical calibration is separate.

The package verifies digital solid geometry, stated clearances and a sampled magnetic range model. It has no structural force prediction, physical build, installed-drive qualification, measured contact/force performance or production-yield result. The +2 mm side-motion scenario is not a permitted-travel or 20 N rating.
