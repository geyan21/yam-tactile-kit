# N1 experimental prototype

Original ENPIRE finger geometry: Wenli Xiao / NVIDIA ENPIRE. Source provenance remains in engineering/docs/GEOMETRY.md.

N1 adds a bare steel sensing fork with two shear webs, two fixed carriers and a separately revised PETG frame. The original TPU and the retained R1 CH2-CH5 interfaces are used. The bridge readout is the unchanged C1.1 PCB. N1 has its own combined firmware and recording identity.

The frame is a faceted derivative of the pinned source STL, not the original author's analytic CAD. CAD, material-around-fastener and numerical checks are digital evidence. No physical assembly, force accuracy, contact reliability, endurance or robot qualification is reported.

Structural meshing uses Gmsh 4.15.2; solution uses CalculiX 2.23. Tool provenance and input decks are retained; the tools themselves are not redistributed.
