# Concept scope and attribution

B2 derives its board envelope and placements from the preserved B1 design and the Wenli Xiao / NVIDIA ENPIRE finger geometry. Original model and source provenance remain documented in engineering/docs/GEOMETRY.md.

B2 is an unrouted board-layout study and dimensional fit witness. It has no PCB fabrication release.
C1 is a new analytical constant-section beam and metal bench coupon. No installed gripper adapter, assembled strain bridge, firmware or qualified force sensor is provided for C1.
The magnetic and strain plots are calculations, not physical measurements or production yield.
