03 / THE DESIGN DECISION

Compare the tradeoffs with measurements.

Read the primary-source review ↗

R1 is the first low-cost experiment. Magnetic, flexible-matrix, optical, and root-force approaches remain candidates. A published paper result is not a measured result on this finger.

CandidateContact coverageWhat must be resolvedBuild status
Selection criteria

Reliable 0.2 N contact detection, useful force feedback through 20 N per finger, intact thin-tip access, repeatable replacement, and acceptable wear across multiple builds.
